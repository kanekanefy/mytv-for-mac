# 行为准则

本项目参考 [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) 制定，适用于 Issues、Pull Request 以及任何以本项目名义出现的交流场合。

## 我们的期望

无论年龄、体型、可见或不可见的残障、种族、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育程度、社会经济地位、国籍、外貌、宗教信仰或性取向，参与者都应该被友善、尊重地对待。

**受欢迎的行为：**
- 对不同意见和经历保持尊重
- 给出建设性的反馈，也能坦然接受别人的反馈
- 对自己的失误负责，出错了愿意道歉
- 把社区/项目的整体利益放在个人得失之前

**不可接受的行为：**
- 人身攻击、侮辱性或贬低性的评论
- 公开或私下的骚扰
- 未经同意公开他人的隐私信息（住址、联系方式等）
- 其他明显不适合在专业/协作场合出现的言行

## 处理方式

如果你遇到或目击了违反准则的行为，可以通过 [Issues](../../issues) 私信提及仓库维护者，或按 [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) 里的私密联系方式说明情况（不方便公开的内容请注明"希望私下沟通"）。维护者会视情况采取提醒、警告、限制互动或封禁等处理方式，处理力度与行为的严重程度、是否重复相关。

## 适用范围

适用于本仓库内的所有互动（Issue、Pull Request、评论），以及任何代表本项目公开露面的场合。

# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) and applies to Issues, Pull Requests, and any space where someone is representing this project.

## What we expect

Regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, religion, or sexual identity and orientation, everyone should be treated with kindness and respect.

**Examples of welcome behavior:**
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving constructive feedback, and accepting it gracefully in return
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and apologizing when needed
- Prioritizing the well-being of the community/project over personal gain

**Examples of unacceptable behavior:**
- Personal attacks, insulting or derogatory comments
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone else's private information (address, contact details, etc.) without consent
- Other conduct clearly inappropriate for a professional/collaborative setting

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this code, you can reach the maintainer privately via a note on an [Issue](../../issues) asking for private follow-up, or through the private contact path described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). The maintainer may respond with anything from a private word of caution to restricting interaction or a ban, depending on severity and whether it's a repeat occurrence.

## Scope

This applies to all interactions within this repository (Issues, Pull Requests, comments) and to any setting where someone is publicly representing this project.
